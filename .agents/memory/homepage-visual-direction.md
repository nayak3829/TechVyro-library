---
name: Homepage visual direction
description: The user-approved visual character for TechVyro's public study experience.
---

Keep the homepage and related public study surfaces warm, editorial, organized, and academically trustworthy. Use parchment-like neutrals, ink-toned structure, restrained archival accents, crafted typography, and subtle motion. Avoid generic dashboard styling, excessive glass effects, repeated gradients, and unsupported promotional claims.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected the Premium Study Platform direction for the advanced homepage upgrade. The intended feeling is a carefully curated study desk students want to return to, not a generic technology portal.

**How to apply:** Preserve this character when extending public-facing homepage sections, PDF/quiz discovery surfaces, and related navigation. Keep real data and clarity ahead of decoration, with equivalent polish in light and dark modes.
